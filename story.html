Our Story

Newland’s was inaugurated on March 16 through the generosity of the benefactors of the Sisters of Mary. It is a school-based coffee shop and a TESDA-registered Barista NC II Assessment Center, providing students with a place to practice their skills and experience working in an actual coffee shop.

Our Baristas are students of the Sisters of Mary School, guided and supervised by their instructors. Every cup they prepare and serve is an opportunity to improve their skills, build confidence, and prepare for future employment in the coffee industry.

Newland’s operates on a non-profit basis, and every purchase helps support the mission of the Sisters of Mary in providing education and vocational training to young people from poor families. When you visit Newland’s Café, you are not just buying coffee—you are helping our students build a better future.

Come, enjoy your favorite drink, support our student baristas, and be part of their journey, one cup at a time!
